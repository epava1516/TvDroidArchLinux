# Third-party code

The Wayland server integration in crates/archtv-compositor started from Smithay's
Smallvil example at f581531d479cf2e3307bafb913c5d2bb12860bb3. The TV policy,
process lifecycle, frame scheduling and test integration are developed here.
The original MIT notice is retained in licenses/Smithay-MIT.txt.

SCTK client integration follows its simple_window example, version 0.21.1.
Its MIT notice is retained in licenses/SCTK-MIT.txt.

Upstream sources:
- https://github.com/Smithay/smithay/tree/f581531d479cf2e3307bafb913c5d2bb12860bb3/smallvil
- https://github.com/Smithay/client-toolkit

The experimental shell renderer links tiny-skia 0.11.4 (BSD-3-Clause) and
fontdue 0.9.5 (MIT alternative chosen). Their notices are retained in licenses/.
It loads the system DejaVu Sans font supplied by ttf-dejavu; the font is not
copied into this repository. Transitive dependency/license packaging is part of H8.

The project-local Graphify skill and references were generated by Graphify 0.9.97.
Its Apache-2.0, MIT and NOTICE files are retained under licenses/Graphify-*.txt.
Graphify is a development tool, not a runtime dependency of the TV session.
